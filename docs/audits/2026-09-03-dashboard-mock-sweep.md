## المكتب — `src/app/dashboard/firm/`

**ملخص الماسح:** Grep of the 4 keyword patterns across src/app/dashboard/firm (recursive, .tsx/.ts only) returned 78 raw hits. Combined with manual inspection of every one of the 50 files in the tree (including files the grep missed because they use plain `const X = [...]` without the literal word 'mock'), I found roughly 45 distinct fake/disabled surfaces; the 40 most significant are reported above, ranked blocking > medium > low. Dropped from the report: a handful of duplicate/lower-signal grep hits that point at the same MOCK_* constant already cited elsewhere (e.g. repeated MOCK_CONTRACTS.filter(...) call sites in contracts/page.tsx, repeated MOCK_CLIENTS.filter(...) calls in clients/page.tsx) — these are the same underlying finding, not new ones. Also excluded: cases/[id]/LegalCanvas.tsx, whose lengthy comment documents that a prior MOCK_NODES/MOCK_EDGES graph was deliberately *removed* (a fix, not a live defect), and fees/page.tsx, team/roles/page.tsx internals, and of-counsel/page.tsx/secondment/new/page.tsx, which were only partially read past their headers given the 40-item cap — flagged as low/unverified rather than omitted silently. By contrast, several pages under team/, departments/, archive/, tasks/, branches/, health-check/, and profile/ are already honestly labeled 'Backend-ready' with an in-page toast admitting the data is local-only, so those are graded medium/already_known rather than blocking.</summary>

## الإدارة — `src/app/dashboard/admin/`

**ملخص الماسح:** Ran the requested grep (107 raw hits) plus follow-up greps for sample/dummy/fake/placeholder/coming-soon and for top-level hardcoded arrays across all ~40 admin route subfolders. Read each hit's surrounding context to separate real findings from noise. Most of the 107 raw grep hits are ordinary `disabled={...}` button states (loading/validation UX) or `placeholder=` input attributes, which are not defects and are excluded. Several files (audit-log, broadcasts, tickets, subscriptions/payments, team) carry deliberate '/* No mock */' or explanatory comments documenting that mock fallbacks were already removed — these are NOT reported as findings (already_known would apply, and they're not currently showing fake data). Reported 21 of the significant findings, none dropped for space (well under the 40 cap) — the list is exhaustive for what a fresh read of each flagged file's top-level data justified. The dominant pattern: an entire tier of financial/ops admin dashboards (revenue, payouts, escrow, disputes, marketplace/orders, security, system logs, reviews, ai-usage + credits + reports, reports) are 100% hardcoded arrays with zero fetch calls — these are the most severe because an admin/owner glancing at them would read real SAR amounts, IBANs, and threat data that are entirely invented.
